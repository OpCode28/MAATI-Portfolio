FIELD SENSING LAYER (SOIL & MICROCLIMATE)

01 • SOIL SENSING

FC-28 / YL-69 Soil Moisture Sensor

Why we use it: “Measures the moisture condition of the soil and provides a key input for irrigation decisions.”

Role: Soil Moisture • Irrigation Input • Field Monitoring
02 • AMBIENT SENSING

DHT11 Environmental Sensor

Why we use it: “Measures temperature and humidity to provide additional environmental context.”

Role: Temperature • Humidity • Environmental Monitoring
03 • PRECIPITATION

Rain Sensor Module

Why we use it: “Provides rain/wet-condition information that can be considered during irrigation decisions.”

Role: Rain Detection • Irrigation Context
04 • LIGHT SENSING

Light Sensor (LDR Module)

Why we use it: “Measures ambient light and adds another environmental parameter to the sensing layer.”

Role: Light Monitoring • Environmental Context

CONTROL, VISION & ACTUATION LAYER

05 • CONTROLLER

ESP32 Dev Board

Why we use it: “Acts as the central embedded controller, collecting sensor data, performing local processing and managing field connectivity.”

Role: Sensor Interface • Local Processing • Connectivity • Control
06 • OPTICAL AI

ESP32-CAM Vision Node

Why we use it: “Captures plant and leaf images to provide the visual information required for computer-vision-based crop analysis.”

Role: Plant Imaging • Crop Monitoring • Computer Vision
07 • SWITCHING

5V Relay Module

Why we use it: “Connects the digital control system to the physical irrigation actuator.”

Role: Switching • Pump Control • Actuation
08 • ACTUATION

Water Pump Actuator

Why we use it: “Converts the irrigation decision into physical water delivery to the crop.”

Role: Water Delivery • Automated Irrigation • Physical Action
